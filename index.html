Overview

Embark on an unforgettable 14-day journey through Rajasthan, the most colorful and culturally rich state of India. From the bustling streets of Old Delhi to the majestic Taj Mahal in Agra, and the sprawling deserts of Jaisalmer, this grand tour covers every iconic landmark.

Stay in heritage havelis, interact with local artisans, enjoy camel safaris at sunset, and witness the architectural brilliance of Rajput forts. Our local experts have meticulously crafted this itinerary to provide a balance of guided sightseeing and relaxed exploration at your own pace.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi

Welcome to India! Upon arrival at Indira Gandhi International Airport, our representative will meet you and transfer you to your hotel. Spend the rest of the day relaxing.

Day 2

Exploring Old & New Delhi

Enjoy a guided tour including a rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk, Jama Masjid, Red Fort, India Gate, and the Qutub Minar.

Day 3

Delhi to Agra

Drive to Agra in the morning. Visit the magnificent Agra Fort and witness the breathtaking sunset at the Taj Mahal.

Day 4

Agra to Jaipur via Fatehpur Sikri

En route to Jaipur, visit the abandoned Mughal city of Fatehpur Sikri. Arrive in Jaipur and relax in your heritage hotel.

Day 5

Jaipur Sightseeing

Spend the day exploring Jaipur, including the Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal, and local colorful bazaars.

Day 6

Jaipur to Bikaner

Drive to the desert city of Bikaner. Visit the impressive Junagarh Fort and walk through the old town's narrow streets.

Day 7

Bikaner to Jaisalmer

Travel deeper into the Thar Desert to reach Jaisalmer, the Golden City. Watch the sunset over the golden fort walls.

Day 8

Jaisalmer & Desert Safari

Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon ki Haveli, and Gadisar Lake. In the evening, head to the Sam Sand Dunes for a camel safari and dinner.

Day 9

Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive to the Blue City, Jodhpur. In the afternoon, explore the local markets around the Clock Tower.

Day 10

Jodhpur Sightseeing

Visit the majestic Mehrangarh Fort, offering panoramic views of the blue city, followed by the white marble Jaswant Thada.

Day 11

Jodhpur to Udaipur via Ranakpur

Drive to Udaipur. En route, stop at Ranakpur to marvel at the intricately carved 15th-century Jain temples. Arrive in the City of Lakes.

Day 12

Udaipur City Tour

Visit the grand City Palace, Jagdish Temple, and Saheliyon Ki Bari. Enjoy a relaxing boat ride on Lake Pichola in the evening.

Day 13

Fly back to Delhi

After breakfast, transfer to Udaipur airport for your domestic flight to New Delhi. Spend the evening shopping or relaxing.

Day 14

Departure

Transfer to the international airport for your onward flight, carrying a suitcase full of unforgettable memories from Rajasthan.
